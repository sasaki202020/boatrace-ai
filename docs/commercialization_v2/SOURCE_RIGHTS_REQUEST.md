# Source Rights Request Draft

This is an unsent draft. It is not permission evidence.

## Proposed limited use

We request a written answer concerning locally obtained pre-race entry files used by an internal research system. No login bypass, automated betting, resale of raw data or external API is proposed. The frozen research model produces derived probabilities. Real predictions will not be published or sold before explicit permission and separate review.

Please identify the organization, department, representative or reference number and answer each item explicitly:

1. Exact files, screens or datasets covered by the answer.
2. Permitted acquisition method, including manual download or automated retrieval.
3. Whether the data may be used as model input.
4. Whether numeric fields may be stored and for how long.
5. Whether raw files may be stored and for how long.
6. Whether derived predictions may be stored internally.
7. Whether derived predictions may be shown publicly.
8. Whether derived predictions may be used in a free beta.
9. Whether commercial subscription use is allowed.
10. Whether an API or partner delivery is allowed.
11. Whether third-party sharing is allowed.
12. Required deletion conditions and retention limits.
13. Request or acquisition limits and retry/parallel limits.
14. Permission start date, end date and revocation conditions.
15. Required attribution or notices.
16. If this use cannot be permitted, the official data/licensing contact or product.

Ambiguous answers such as “reasonable use”, “probably acceptable” or an unanswered item will remain `UNVERIFIED`.
