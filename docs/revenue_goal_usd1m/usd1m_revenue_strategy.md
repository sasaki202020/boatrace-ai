# USD 1M Revenue Strategy

Date: 2026-05-10

## Boundary

This plan does not monetize gambling advice, betting signals, or regulated financial advice. The safest monetizable asset is the engineering capability demonstrated in this workspace: public-data ingestion, freshness checks, missing-state handling, reproducible reports, monitoring, and audit trails.

## Selected Route

Route 1 is highest expectation because it uses the existing workspace as direct proof, avoids betting-advice risk, and supports high-ticket B2B pricing.

Revenue path examples:
- 40 sprints x USD 15,000 = USD 600,000, plus 160 monitor-months x USD 2,500 = USD 400,000.
- 10 clients paying USD 2,500/mo for 12 months = USD 300,000, plus 47 sprints x USD 15,000 = USD 705,000.

## Offer

Public Data Reliability Sprint:
- Diagnose public-data pipelines for freshness, schema drift, missing states, replayability, and reporting gaps.
- Deliver an audit report, reproducible checks, lightweight dashboard, and daily/weekly monitoring outputs.
- Excludes regulated advice, scraping that violates terms, spam, gambling predictions, legal/tax work, and paid deployment without approval.

## 30-Day Validation Plan

1. Package the offer and landing page.
2. Build a manual target list by company segment, not personal data scraping.
3. Send only targeted, relevant, low-volume outreach.
4. Success metric: 20 qualified contacts, 5 replies or calls, 1 paid diagnostic.
5. If no paid diagnostic, refine segment and pain statement before building software.

## Missing Decisions

- Weekly time budget for sales and delivery.
- Preferred language and geography: Japan only, global English, or both.
- Legal entity, invoicing, tax handling, and contract template.
- Whether the current repo can be used publicly as anonymized proof.
- Minimum acceptable project price.
- Whether outbound email/LinkedIn outreach is approved.
